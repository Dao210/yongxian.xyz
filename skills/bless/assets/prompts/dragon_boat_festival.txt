# 端午节图像生成提示词

## 风格定位
动感有力，突出竞渡与坚守的精神

## 元素库

### 竞渡元素
- Paddles in motion (showing rhythm)
- Water spray/splashes
- Boat shapes (simplified, not ornate)
- Dragon heads (abstract suggestion)

### 传统元素
- Zongzi (pyramid-shaped rice dumplings)
- Bamboo leaves (as wrapping material)
- Realgar wine (traditional, shown subtly)
- Mugwort/Calamus (hanging herbs)

### 自然元素
- Flowing water (river)
- Summer greenery
- Morning light
- Rain (Drum Boy beating rain legend)

### 人物元素
- Paddling teams (synchronized motion)
- Solo figure by water (contemplation)
- Hands wrapping zongzi (craft)

## 色彩指导

### 主色
- Deep river blue
- Fresh leaf green
- Clean white

### 点缀色
- Cinnabar red (dragon heads, drums)
- Gold (sunlight on water)
- Brown (zongzi leaves)

### 避免
- Overwhelming red
- Dark/gloomy colors
- Clashing bright colors

## 构图原则

1. **动势** - 对角线构图表现速度和力量
2. **节奏** - 重复元素形成视觉节奏（船桨、水波）
3. **焦点** - 单一清晰焦点（龙舟队、屈原像、粽子的中心）
4. **流动** - 线条引导视线流动

## 负面提示词

```
NO: cute cartoon characters (not serious enough),
NO: overly ornate traditional dragon boats (too old-fashioned),
NO: chaotic crowds (focus on unity and order),
NO: text or poems in image,
NO: gore or dramatic death scenes (for Qu Yuan reference),
NO: rainy storm scenes (unless subtle, focus on the spirit)
```

## 技术规格

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (wide for boat scenes) or 4:3
- Resolution: minimum 1024x1024
- Style: dynamic illustration / modern art / stylized photography
- Mood: energetic, determined, culturally proud
