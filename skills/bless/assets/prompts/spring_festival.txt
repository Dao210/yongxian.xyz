# 春节图像生成提示词

## 风格定位
现代简约风格，融合传统元素与当代审美

## 核心提示词

### 选项1: 温暖家庭场景
```
A warm Chinese New Year family dinner scene, modern minimalist style.
Round table with traditional dishes, soft warm lighting from hanging red lanterns.
In the background: subtle spring couplet calligraphy on clean white walls.
Through the window: a glimpse of winter evening sky with first stars.
Color palette: warm reds and golds as accents, not overwhelming.
Atmosphere: intimate, peaceful, hopeful.
Style: contemporary watercolor illustration, clean lines, generous white space.
No text in image.
```

### 选项2: 成长与希望
```
A symbolic illustration of new beginnings for Chinese New Year.
A single sprout emerging from winter soil, protected by gentle hands.
In the background: abstract suggestion of mountains (journey ahead) and a sunrise.
Subtle red paper cut art style decoration in corners.
Color palette: earth tones with touches of auspicious red.
Mood: quiet, determined, full of potential.
Style: minimalist digital art, inspired by Chinese ink painting but modern.
No text in image.
```

### 选项3: 城市中的温暖
```
A modern city apartment during Chinese New Year eve.
A person at window, looking at city lights with a warm cup of tea.
Indoor: subtle red lantern string lights, a clean paper-cut on window.
Outdoor: city skyline with distant fireworks (small, not overwhelming).
A sense of peace amid celebration.
Style: Studio Ghibli inspired - warm, detailed, emotionally resonant.
Color: warm indoor glow contrasting with cool blue night outside.
No text in image.
```

## 元素库（可组合使用）

### 灯光元素
- Soft lantern glow (warm, not harsh red)
- Candlelight on dinner table
- First stars of the year
- Window warm light from buildings

### 传统元素（现代诠释）
- Paper cuts (minimalist, not ornate)
- Spring couplets (abstract suggestion, not full text)
- Red envelopes (artistic arrangement, not scattered)
- Bamboo (symbol of resilience and growth)

### 自然元素
- Sprout/new growth
- Winter branches with first buds
- Snow (if applicable to region)
- Dawn light

### 人物元素
- Silhouette or back view
- Hands (holding, preparing, protecting)
- Reflection in window
- Shadow on wall

## 色彩指导

### 主色
- Warm white/cream (base)
- Soft gray (modern touch)

### 点缀色（谨慎使用）
- Cinnabar red (not bright red)
- Antique gold (not yellow gold)
- Deep navy (for contrast)

### 避免
- Bright neon colors
- Overwhelming red (use as accent only)
- Clashing colors

## 构图原则

1. **留白** - 至少30%留白，避免拥挤
2. **焦点** - 单一清晰焦点，不分散
3. **层次** - 前景中景背景分明
4. **平衡** - 视觉重量均衡

## 负面提示词（需要避免）

```
NO: overwhelming red, gaudy decorations, crowded composition,
NO: text, Chinese characters, busy patterns,
NO: traditional realistic painting style (too old-fashioned),
NO: cartoon/anime style (not serious enough),
NO: overly ornate details (not modern),
NO: dragons, lions, or mythical creatures (unless specifically requested),
NO: fireworks as main element (use as subtle background only)
```

## 技术规格

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3
- Resolution: minimum 1024x1024
- Style: digital illustration / watercolor / minimalist art
- Mood: warm, hopeful, peaceful
