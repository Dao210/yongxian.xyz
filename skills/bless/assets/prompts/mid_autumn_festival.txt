# 中秋节图像生成提示词

## 风格定位
宁静致远，突出月圆与思念的情感连接

## 元素库

### 月亮元素
- Full moon (prominent but not overwhelming)
- Moonlight reflection (on water, window, or tea)
- Moon phases (subtle background pattern)

### 传统元素（现代诠释）
- Mooncakes (artistic arrangement, not pile)
- Osmanthus flowers (delicate, scattered)
- Lanterns (hanging or floating, subtle)
- Tea ceremony elements (cup, pot, steam)

### 人物元素
- Gazing at moon
- Holding tea cup
- Family gathering
- Silhouette against moon

### 自然元素
- Night sky with stars
- Calm water (reflection)
- Autumn leaves (subtle)
- Osmanthus in bloom

## 色彩指导

### 主色
- Deep midnight blue
- Moonlight silver/white
- Warm gold (indoor light)

### 点缀色
- Pale yellow (mooncakes)
- Soft orange (lanterns)
- Jade green (subtle accent)

### 避免
- Bright daylight colors
- Harsh reds (not moon festival appropriate)
- Neon colors

## 构图原则

1. **月亮位置** - 通常在上方1/3处或作为背景元素
2. **留白** - 大量天空留白，营造空灵意境
3. **对角线** - 可用月光、视线形成对角线构图
4. **层次** - 前景人物/物体，中景活动，远景月亮

## 负面提示词

```
NO: cute cartoon rabbits (too childish),
NO: overly ornate palace scenes (too traditional),
NO: busy streets or crowds (breaks the quiet mood),
NO: text, poems, or calligraphy in image,
NO: daylight scenes (must be night),
NO: fireworks (not appropriate for quiet moon appreciation)
```

## 技术规格

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 3:4 (portrait works well for moon)
- Resolution: minimum 1024x1024
- Style: illustration / watercolor / digital art
- Mood: serene, contemplative, warm
